Our Roots

Rooted in Craft, Driven by Passion

Founded in 2021 by a team of dedicated baristas and roasters, Roast & Ground began with a humble mission: make barista-grade specialty coffee approachable, sustainable, and remarkably fresh for home brewers.

We believe great coffee is not an accident. It is the result of respect for the soil, fair treatment for farm workers, and disciplined roasting profiles that reveal the natural sweetness and character hidden inside every raw bean.
