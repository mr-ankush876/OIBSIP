About Roast & Ground

From high-elevation shaded slopes in the Western Ghats to a fragrant morning cup in your home, discover the artisans and ethics behind our roastery.

Our Roots

Rooted in Craft, Driven by Passion

Founded in 2021 by a collective of certified Q-graders and veteran baristas in Bengaluru, Roast & Ground was born from a singular passion: showcase India's extraordinary shade-grown Arabica coffees with the respect and roasting discipline they deserve.

India produces some of the most complex, naturally sweet coffee beans in the world. By bypassing commodity brokers and working directly with family estates in Chikmagalur, Coorg, and Araku Valley, we ensure farmers receive honest, premium compensation while our customers receive beans at the zenith of their freshness.

Guiding Principles

The Three Pillars of Our Roastery

Every decision we make at Roast & Ground is measured against these foundational values.

⚖

1. Fair Farmer Partnerships

We pay 35% above Fairtrade minimums directly to the families who cultivate our coffee, supporting sustainable farming communities across South India.

☕

2. Roasting as an Art

We never roast in bulk. Small 10kg batches allow our roasters to continuously taste, cup, and adjust burner flames for optimal sweetness and clarity.

🌱

3. Environmental Respect

From shade-grown organic agroforestry that protects bird sanctuaries in Coorg to plant-based compostable pouches, nature comes first.
